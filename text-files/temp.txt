A: Corrupted Paperwork

Scattered papers 




Ah crap! Some pranksters
stole the growth hormone
syringes from our research
facility, and injected
them into a Tigrex we had
captured! The beast escaped
and is wreaking havoc!

This contract is seemingly
capable of crossing realms.
Emotions of fear and hunger
are deeply embedded into
the white ink. Reading it
makes you feel sick.
The guild seems unaware of
its existence...
